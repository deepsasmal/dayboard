Your data

Everything lives in this browser. Export a backup now and then, and import it anywhere.

Merge keeps what you have and adds what is new. Replace swaps it all for the file.

Automatic backup

Once a day, if anything changed, a backup file is posted to your own Discord channel. In Discord: channel settings, Integrations, Webhooks, New webhook, Copy URL. This sends your tasks and notes to Discord, so use a private channel.

Outlook meetings

Shows your next meeting under the greeting and puts meetings in the calendar. In Outlook on the web: Settings, Calendar, Shared calendars, Publish a calendar, then paste the ICS link here. Dayboard only reads it. The link stays on this device and is never exported.

Recover

Deleted notes and tasks stay here for 30 days.

    A snapshot is saved at the first open of each day. Restoring only brings back what is missing now.
